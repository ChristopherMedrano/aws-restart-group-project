# Weekly Integration Schedule

**Related issue:** E1-I01
**Status:** Ready for review

| Checkpoint | Purpose | Required input | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Friday, 1:30 PM | Weekly team integration review: confirm scope, owners, dependencies, blockers, and gate progress. | Epic issue, child issues, risk register, and evidence. | Updated priorities, dependency status, and action items. |
| Continuous | Integrate small, reviewed PRs into `master`. | PR review and relevant test evidence. | Deployable trunk. |
| Mid-week | Review cross-team handoffs and emerging risks. | Architecture/API/frontend/QA handoffs. | Blocker and risk updates. |
| End of week | Evaluate exit-gate evidence and demo readiness. | QA report, linked evidence, open blockers. | Accepted/not accepted gate decision. |

The team meets once a week on Fridays starting at 1:30 PM. Confirm the
communication channel at the first formal integration review.
